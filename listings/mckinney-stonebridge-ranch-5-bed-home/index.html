The home, room by room

Entry, study and formal dining

The entry has soaring ceilings, plantation shutters and a ceramic-tile floor. Just off the entry are a secondary bedroom with a full bath, a private study, and a formal dining room with a butler's pantry. A staircase with wrought-iron balusters is the focal point as you move toward the center of the home.

Family room

The family room has a vaulted ceiling, a corner stone fireplace (gas, per the MLS) and a wall of windows that look out to the backyard and covered patio. The MLS also lists an open floor plan, built-in features, a chandelier and decorative lighting.

Kitchen and breakfast area

The open kitchen has a large island, a breakfast bar, plenty of cabinetry and a walk-in pantry. Listed appliances: dishwasher, disposal, electric oven, gas cooktop, microwave, and the kitchen is plumbed for gas. The breakfast area next to the kitchen opens to the covered patio.

Primary suite

The primary bedroom overlooks the backyard. Its ensuite bath has dual vanities, a separate shower, a garden tub and a generous walk-in closet.

Upstairs

Upstairs there is an oversized game room, three more bedrooms and the third full bath.

Laundry, garage and outdoor space

Laundry is in a utility room with a full-size washer and dryer area and both gas and electric dryer hookups. Floors are carpet and ceramic tile. The two-car garage faces the front, has side-by-side parking, a single door and an opener. Outside, the fenced backyard has a covered patio, and the lot is landscaped with a sprinkler system. There is no pool.

Neighborhood and schools

Stonebridge Ranch is a large master-planned community in McKinney, the county seat of Collin County north of Dallas. The listing remarks name these community amenities: an aquatic center, a beach-style pool, playgrounds, miles of hike-and-bike trails, tennis and pickleball courts, sand volleyball and ponds. The remarks also note nearby H-E-B and Trader Joe's stores and access to State Highway 121 toward DFW Airport.

School district: McKinney ISD, according to the listing. Campus assignments can change, so confirm the schools for this home directly with McKinney ISD. HOA dues were not included in the data we have; ask the listing agent for the current amount.
